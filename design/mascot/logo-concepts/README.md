# Dusty logo explorations

These are two *candidate* freedisk.space identities for review. The active website and app branding are unchanged.

| Direction | Best use | Files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 — Dusty peeks over the existing stack | Website wordmark, larger printed use | `01-dusty-peek-wordmark.svg`, `01-dusty-peek-mark.svg` |
| 02 — Dusty and clear space | App icon, favicon, compact brand placements | `02-dusty-clearspace-wordmark.svg`, `02-dusty-clearspace-mark.svg`, `02-dusty-clearspace-small.svg` |

Open `compare-both.svg` for a side-by-side view. All artwork is vector. Direction 01 uses Dusty's original shape generator and palette; direction 02 retains the asymmetric upright-left/flopped-right ears while reducing the figure to a compact mark. Neither is ready to replace the live logo until a direction and small-size behavior are chosen.

Regenerate: `python3 design/mascot/logo-concepts/build.py` from the repository root.
